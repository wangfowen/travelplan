## Lahaina status (post-Aug 2023 fire) — summary

Front Street oceanfront row north of the burn zone (behind Lahaina Cannery Mall) is open: Aloha Mixed Plate, Star Noodle (relocated), Honu Oceanside, Māla Ocean Tavern, Coco Deck, Old Lahaina Lūʻau. Kimo's announced return to 730 Front St (Chan Wa Building), no date. ʻUlu o Lele interim marketplace (24 businesses/food trucks, former Outlets of Maui site) grand opening **Nov 10, 2026** — shops open as ready. Sources: MNKO-LAH; HMLAH; https://mauinow.com/2026/07/16/kimos-returning-to-front-street-marking-new-chapter-for-lahaina-restaurant/ ; https://mauinow.com/2026/11/05/24-businesses-selected-for-lahainas-%CA%BBulu-o-lele-marketplace-ahead-of-nov-10-grand-opening/ ; https://thehawaiivacationguide.com/best-restaurants-lahaina/ (2026 what's-open guide; non-aggregator blog, use only for status).

## Everyday institutions / local-food tier

- **Sam Sato's** (Wailuku, since 1933) — dry mein (saimin-style noodles, dashi on the side; recipe from a Chinese cook in the Puʻunēnē era), saimin, chow funn, manju from bakery side, plate lunches — hawaiʻi magazine (HM15; HMCHEAP #5; HMRC26 plate lunch #5, noodle #3, cheap eats #5) — https://www.hawaiimagazine.com/blogs/hawaii_today/2014/1/3/maui_sam_satos_dry_mein_noodle ; Hawaii Herald https://www.thehawaiiherald.com/2014/06/24/maui-omiyage/ — 1750 Wili Pa Loop; Mon–Sat ~7am–2pm (2014 hours, re-verify).
- **Tin Roof** (Kahului; Sheldon Simeon's takeout counter, ~10 min from airport) — mochiko chicken, fat chow funn with pork, pork belly bowl, chocolate butter mochi — hawaiʻi magazine (HM15; HMCHEAP; HMRC26 plate lunch #2, noodle #2, cheap eats #2) — links above.
- **T. Komoda Store & Bakery** (Makawao, family-run since 1916) — doughnut sticks, cream puffs (vanilla custard), long johns; sells out early — hawaiʻi magazine (HM15; HMRC26 bakery #3); Hawaii Herald — 3674 Baldwin Ave.
- **Home Maid Bakery** (Wailuku, Lower Main St; Kahului deli outlet on Dairy Rd per 2014) — crispy manju (omiyage staple), since 1960 (Kozuki family) — hawaii herald — https://www.thehawaiiherald.com/2014/06/24/maui-omiyage/ — current location/outlets need verification.
- **Four Sisters Bakery & Catering** (Wailuku, Vineyard St) — hot butter rolls, often sold out by afternoon — hawaiʻi magazine (HM15).
- **Tasaka Guri Guri** (Kahului, Maui Mall) — guri-guri sherbet, strawberry and pineapple only — hawaiʻi magazine (HM15).
