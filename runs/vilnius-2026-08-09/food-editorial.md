## Named local food press

**Vilnius In Your Pocket** (in your pocket):
- Etno Dvaras (Pilies St) — "jolly, warm-hearted, meat-and-potatoes"; first Vilnius restaurant certified by the Gastronomic Heritage Foundation for traditional local-origin cooking — https://www.inyourpocket.com/vilnius/vilnius-best-restaurants_75478f
- Lokys — 15th-c. merchant's cellar (opened as a restaurant 1972), game specialist: wild boar, roe deer, venison, quail, beaver stew
- Sweet Root (Užupis) — modern/seasonal reinterpretation of Lithuanian produce
- Alaus Biblioteka — "best sophisticated craft beer bar" — https://www.inyourpocket.com/vilnius/vilnius-best-craft-beer_75481f
- Šnekutis (3 locations) — "probably the best bar in Vilnius," stuffs its own cepelinai
- Bambalynė — ~100 Lithuanian microbrewery beers, medieval cellar

**LRT English** — other pieces:
- Čeburekinė (Šopeno St, open since 2009) — famous for chebureki (Crimean Tatar fried meat/cheese pastry), also cepelinai/steaks/soups — lrt — https://www.lrt.lt/en/news-in-english/19/1275738/famous-vilnius-cafe-draws-locals-and-foreigners-with-tatar-pastries
- Toranis (Persian, Pilies St) — opened by a home cook who previously hosted Persian meals for friends — lrt, from "#Flames. World cuisine in Vilnius"
- Note: LRT coverage confirms Falstaff and Gault&Millau have begun reviewing Lithuanian restaurants and Gault&Millau opened a Vilnius regional office — signal that the scene is professionalizing, not itself a source of named entries.

**visitvilnius.lt** (tourism board, citable structured guide — not independent press):
- Traditional-Lithuanian roundup: Etno Dvaras, Šnekutis, Senoji Trobelė ("styled as a traditional country house," refined — note reddit's price/quality pushback in reddit.md), Stikliai Aludė (cellar tavern, local beer), Forto Dvaras (chain, consistent), Aline Leičiai (house-brewed mead), Bernelių Užeiga (folk interior, big portions), Katpėdėlė (budget), Gabi (Pilies St courtyard, romantic), Lokys

- Cepelinai: Žilvino Restoranas and Žvejų smuklė called out as cheap, hearty spots
