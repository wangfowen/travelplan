## Neighborhoods / winter weather by side
- Weather: south (Poʻipū/Kōloa) driest & sunniest, ~32 in/yr; north (Princeville >60 in/yr; Hanalei ~85 in/yr, Dec ~12–15 in — numbers inconsistent across sources); east (Kapaʻa/Wailua/Līhuʻe) in between, Wailua ~46 in/yr; Poʻipū can still get multi-day winter downpours — makana charters https://makanacharters.com/kauai-south-shore-vs-north-shore/ ; fodors forum https://www.fodors.com/community/united-states/january-trip-to-kauai-994039/ ; lovebigisland https://www.lovebigisland.com/kauai/weather/
- Poʻipū/Kōloa (south) — driest; condo/resort strip + Old Kōloa Town (Big Save market, shops); Poʻipū Beach Park, Shipwreck/Makawehi bluff walk (Māhāʻulepū Heritage Trail) for running; car required; best winter swimming side; tradeoff: resort-y, 40 min from north shore — my pick (weather: above)
- Kapaʻa/Wailua (east) — real town, Kapaʻa Foodland/Safeway, Ke Ala Hele Makalae coastal bike/run path, bus mainline; mid rain; hotel rates a bit lower; tradeoff: Kūhiō Hwy traffic through Kapaʻa ("Kapaʻa crawl"), some roadside motels noisy — govisithawaii https://www.govisithawaii.com/kauai-where-to-stay/ ; rest my pick
- Hanalei/Princeville (north) — most beautiful; Princeville = golf/condo planned community with Foodland; Hanalei small town; tradeoff in Dec: wettest side, unswimmable surf, Hanalei Bridge flood closures can strand you (3 closures Sept–Oct 2026), ʻAnini water notice — sources above; my pick
- Līhuʻe — county seat, airport, Costco/Target/Times, Kalapakī Bay (Kauai Marriott), central for day trips both directions; tradeoff: least scenic, commercial — my pick
- Hanapēpē/Waimea (west) — driest/hottest, small towns, Port Allen boat base, gateway to Waimea Canyon (mostly closed); few rentals — my pick

## Practical
- Ocean safety: Kauai has long had the highest per-capita ocean-drowning rate in Hawaii (10-yr fatal drowning rate 82.5/100k vs Oahu 27.2, DOH, undated); 40 drownings on Kauai 2020–2024 (state Water Safety Plan); ~69% of Hawaii ocean drowning victims are visitors (74% on Kauai, older study); snorkeling the top activity in visitor drownings (DOH Aug 2024) — beat of hawaii https://beatofhawaii.com/69-of-hawaii-drownings-are-visitors-as-maui-rate-doubles/ ; civil beat https://www.civilbeat.org/2017/08/hawaii-tourists-are-drowning-at-nine-times-the-rate-of-locals/ ; DOH https://oceansafety.hawaii.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/non_resident-August-2024-by-DOH-EMSIPSB.pdf
  - Swim at lifeguarded beaches, ask the guard; Ocean Safety Bureau 808-241-4984 — kauairecovers (above). Note: Beat of Hawaii is a blog-ish outlet; cite the Water Safety Plan figures it reports.
- Winter rain pattern: Nov–Mar wet season; trades bring rain to north/east, Kona storms can soak the south; flash-flood warnings frequent (three Hanalei closures Sept–Oct 2026); don't cross swollen streams — kauairecovers + sources above; pattern my pick
- Brown-water advisories after heavy rain: check DOH viewer — kauairecovers (above)
