# Kauai: food editorial track (gathered 2026-11-05)

Format: name | area | what it is | tags | link | detail.
Area key: E = east (Līhuʻe, Puhi, Wailua, Kapaʻa); S = south (Kōloa, Poʻipū, Lāwaʻi, Kalāheo); W = west (Hanapēpē, ʻEleʻele/Port Allen, Waimea); N = north (Kīlauea, Princeville, Hanalei).
`(area: my knowledge)` means the source did not state the town; it comes from my own knowledge. `area?` means not confirmed.

Tag abbreviations. HM RC26 = Hawaiʻi Magazine 2026 Readers' Choices (published 2026-05-12, top 5 per category): https://www.hawaiimagazine.com/2026-readers-choices-awards-kauai/ . HM RC25 = 2025 edition: https://www.hawaiimagazine.com/2025-readers-choices-awards-kauai/ . Readers' polls skew toward resort restaurants; record them as provenance only.

## Source coverage and absences (for the brief's callouts)

- **Michelin:** no Hawaii guide exists. Honolulu Magazine says Michelin does not send inspectors to Hawaiʻi (https://www.honolulumagazine.com/?p=41224). 2026 legislature resolution SCR2 asked HTA to sponsor a guide; status "Introduced – Dead, 2026-02-13" (https://legiscan.com/HI/text/SCR2/2026). Zero Kauai entries.
- **50 Best (World / 51–100 / North America / Bars):** no Kauai entries. **50 Best Discovery:** a domain-restricted search found no Kauai venue. The only Hawaiʻi Discovery listings surfaced were Big Island hotels: Kona Village (https://www.theworlds50best.com/discovery/Establishments/US/Kailua-Kona/Kona-Village-A-Rosewood-Resort.html) and Mauna Kea Beach Hotel. Treat Kauai as absent.
- **World of Mouth:** `worldofmouth.app/destinations/kauai` returns 404, and a domain-restricted search found no Kauai restaurant pages. Absent.
- **Eater:** eater.com refuses the search tool's user agent (API error). The general search found no Eater Kauai guide; Eater has no Hawaiʻi edition. Absence noted, though the domain block means this was not fully checked.
- **James Beard:** the only Kauai honour found is Hamura Saimin, America's Classics 2006. No Kauai chef appeared as a semifinalist in the search; recent Hawaiʻi semifinalists are on Maui, Oʻahu and the Big Island (e.g. Michele Di Bari, Lahaina, 2026: https://mauinow.com/2026/01/23/lahaina-chef-named-james-beard-award-semifinalist/).
- **Culinary Backstreets:** does not cover Hawaiʻi (my knowledge; not searched).
- **Local press used:** Hawaiʻi Magazine (Readers' Choices 2025/2026, plate lunch 2025, shave ice 2024, older guides); Honolulu Magazine (Hale ʻĀina 2026, Kauai guides 2022 and earlier, beer); Kauai Now and The Garden Island (2025–26 openings and closures). Neither Kauai Now nor The Garden Island has a "best of" food list that I found; they carry openings news only.
