# Kauai — Where to stay: decisions (2026-11-05)

Source: kauai/dossier/city.md only. Reddit stay threads unavailable this run (extension-level block), so the only secondary source is missing. Every neighborhood characterization in the dossier is tagged "my pick" except the weather numbers, the Kapaʻa note from govisithawaii, and the north-shore closure facts.

## Shortlist
1. **Kapaʻa / Wailua (east).** It best passes the "live here for a week" test: a real town with two supermarkets (Foodland, Safeway), the Ke Ala Hele Makalae paved seaside path for running, the bus mainline (reduced service), and moderate rain (~46 in/yr). Tradeoffs: the Kapaʻa crawl on Kūhiō Hwy and noisy roadside motels. Listed first because it is the most like daily life.
2. **Poʻipū / Kōloa (south).** The driest side (~32 in/yr), with the only reliably swimmable December sea. Daily life works: Big Save in Kōloa and the Māhāʻulepū bluff trail. Tradeoffs: it feels like a resort, it needs a car, and it is ~40 min from the north shore.
3. **Hanalei, flagged "avoid in December."** This is a genuine booking trap: it looks idyllic, but there were 3 Hanalei Bridge flood closures in Sept–Oct 2026, each stranding everything west of the bridge, plus ~85 in/yr of rain and unswimmable winter surf. Princeville is noted as the connected alternative, but it shares the rain and has the ʻAnini water notice, so I did not recommend it as a base.

## Split line
- Plan A, 9 nights: split ~5 Poʻipū + ~4 Kapaʻa. The dossier supports this: weather by side, Port Allen as the winter boat base, and the Hāʻena shuttle from Waipā/Princeville running 7:20am onward with a 6:30am first parking slot, which is a long drive from Poʻipū.
- Plan B, 6 nights: stay in one base. The choice depends on priority. This is a judgment call and is not sourced.

## Cut list
- **Līhuʻe:** cut. It is commercial and the least scenic, and the dossier's case for it is central location and proximity to sights, which are not criteria. Its big-box shopping (Costco, Target) is reachable from Kapaʻa anyway.
- **Hanapēpē / Waimea (west):** cut. It has few rentals, it is the gateway to the mostly closed Waimea Canyon and Kōkeʻe, and the dossier has no daily-life facts for it (no grocery, no running route).
- **Princeville as its own entry:** folded into the Hanalei flag. It is a planned golf/condo community that is not walkable and is the wettest side, so it does not pass the week-living test in December.
- **Kīlauea town:** cut. The dossier has no neighborhood facts for it.

## Gaps / not confirmed
- **Christmas-week availability and prices:** not in the dossier. The fragment carries a "book early" note labeled as own knowledge.
- **Gyms:** no data for any area, so no gym is named.
- **Rainfall figures:** Hanalei and Princeville numbers are inconsistent across sources. I used Hanalei ~85 in/yr; Dec ~12–15 in is unconfirmed.
- **"~40 min Poʻipū to north shore"** is the dossier's my-pick estimate. The Kapaʻa→Hanalei drive time is not in the dossier.
