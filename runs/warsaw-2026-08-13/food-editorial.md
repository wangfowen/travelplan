## Native food formats

### Bar mleczny (milk bars — cheap Polish cafeteria food)
- **Bar Mleczny Lindleya 14** — called "Warsaw's best milk bar" by one source after testing multiple locations — [localpress] — thefabryk.com blog
- **Bar Mleczny Familijny** — centrally located on Nowy Świat, Polish cuisine staples, meals 7–20 PLN — [localpress] — Tripadvisor review coverage
- **Sztuka Mleka** — Grzybowska 30, retro charm + contemporary vibe, fresh take on Polish classics — [localpress] — warsawinsider.pl
- **Bar Mleczny Mokotowska 22** — stylish milk bar, classic Polish dishes, modern interior — [localpress] — warsawinsider.pl
- **Bar Bambino** — one of the most famous milk bars, classic Polish dishes since the 1950s — [localpress] — warsawinsider.pl; also cited as a zapiekanka spot separately
- **Bar Mleczny Rusałka** — Praga district, next to a church — [localpress] — warsawinsider.pl
- Milk bars as a category also covered by The Culture Trip ("The Most Historic Milk Bars ('Bar Mlecznys') In Poland") and Robert Simonson's Substack "The Milk Bars of Warsaw" — [localpress] — https://theculturetrip.com/europe/poland/articles/the-most-historic-milk-bars-or-bar-mlecznys-in-poland ; https://robertsimonson.substack.com/p/the-milk-bars-of-warsaw

- **Gościniec Polskie Pierogi** — named among top pierogi spots — [localpress] — najlepszewwarszawie.com

- **Prodiż Warszawski** — named among top pierogi spots — [localpress] — najlepszewwarszawie.com
- **Pierogarnia na Bednarskiej** — named among top pierogi spots — [localpress] — najlepszewwarszawie.com

### Zapiekanka (street food open sandwich)
- **Zapiexy Luxusowe** — avoids tourist spots, fresh-made, decent prices, outdoor seating — [localpress] — Tripadvisor review
- **Zapiekanki Wisła** — Powiśle district, premium casseroles, stylish restaurant-like interior, 9 versions incl. vegan, bread from local artisan bakery — [localpress] — bestofwarsaw.pl
- **Z Pół Matra Cięte** — Praga Północ, oven-baked casseroles (2 sizes), crispy baguette/butter/mushrooms/cheddar-mozzarella blend, also rotisserie chicken thighs and homemade ice cream — [localpress] — bestofwarsaw.pl
- **Zapiekanki u Pandy (Panda's Casseroles)** — Bielany Town Hall booth, ~300m from Stare Bielany metro — [localpress] — bestofwarsaw.pl

### Other Polish street food
- **Pyzy Flaki Gorące** — old Praga district, "Warsaw's original street food" pyzy (round potato dumplings), toppings from traditional pork crackling to saffron milk cap pesto — [localpress] — search results
- **Warsaw Window (Okno)** fries — iconic Warsaw fries spot, first location on Polna Street open 12 years — [localpress] — search results
- Zapiekanka as a category described generally as one of the city's most popular street foods, widely available especially late at night — [localpress] — search results
