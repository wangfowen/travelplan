- The Dollhouse, Games & Toys Museum — 150+ historic dollhouses/miniature interiors (kitchens, pharmacies, classrooms), some 200+ years old — `warsaw in your pocket` — https://www.inyourpocket.com/poland/warsaw/articles/a-hidden-treasure-in-warsaw-inside-the-dollhouse
- Museum of Warsaw — permanent exhibit "Warsaw Things," 22 thematic rooms, visitor picks one of 4 paths through the museum — `warsaw in your pocket` — https://inyourpocket.com/warsaw/museum-of-warsaw-ordinary-objects-extraordinary_78454f
- Bridge construction open-air museum — world's first, steel fragments from 3 historic Warsaw bridges (Kierbedź 1864, Citadel Bridge 1875, Poniatowski 1914) — `my pick source: muzea.waw.pl` — https://muzea.waw.pl/en/guides/unusual-museums-warsaw/
- Fragrance Workshop, Antonina Leśniewska Museum of Pharmacy — Wednesdays 2:30-5:30pm, make fragrances from natural essential oils — HANDS-ON, fits traveler profile — `muzea.waw.pl`
- Museum of Polish Vodka — blend-your-own nalewka station + guided tastings included with admission — HANDS-ON, fits traveler profile — `muzea.waw.pl`

## Neighborhoods
- **Praga** (Praga-Północ / Praga-Południe) — right bank, historic, undergoing revitalization/gentrification, artists and young professionals, more affordable than center; Ząbkowska Street is the "coolest street" — alternative bars/cafés — source: theculturetrip.com
- **Powiśle** — most livable slice of central Warsaw, river access, cafés, walkable "village-like" atmosphere, transformed from working-class to one of the coolest districts — good candidate for "could live here for a week" test
- **Mokotów** (Stary Mokotów / Sielce) — good transport + green areas, tree-lined streets, parks, established schools, strong local identity
- **Żoliborz** (Stary Żoliborz / Żoliborz Oficerski) — quiet, leafy, pre-war character, tight-knit community, suited for quiet focus
- Sources are real-estate/expat-guide sites (investropa, dobralokacja, expatarrivals, theculturetrip) — no single named authority; treat as `my pick` composite unless corroborated on Reddit.

## Transit and practical
- **Fare system**: integrated ZTM ticket valid on metro + trams + buses + SKM trains within zone 1, free transfers during validity — 2026 prices: single 75-min ticket 4.40 PLN (2.20 PLN discounted); 90-min zone 1+2 ticket 7.00 PLN; 24h tourist ticket 15.00 PLN; 72h tourist ticket 36.00 PLN; 30-day pass 110.00 PLN — source: warsawmetro.org/en/tickets, 2026
- **Payment**: station vending machines, SMS, mobiWAWA app, or tap contactless bank card/phone directly on metro/tram (Open Payment, auto-charged)
- **Ride-hailing apps operating in Warsaw**: Uber (broad coverage, multiple tariffs — UberX/Comfort/Green), Bolt (highest ride volume, cheapest/fastest pickup), FreeNow (business-oriented, fixed-price pre-booking) — all three active citywide
- **Chopin Airport → city center**:
  - SKM train (S2/S3) — fastest, ~20 min, 4.40 PLN, every 20-30 min
  - Bus 175 — connects to Central Railway Station + Old Town, 30-45 min, 4.40 PLN, every 15-20 min
  - Official taxi — 50-65 PLN, 20-30 min
  - Uber/FreeNow — ~40-55 PLN, 20-35 min
  - Airport is ~10km from city center
  - source: multiple 2026 travel-guide sites, cross-checked figures consistent across grandpl.com, welcomepickups.com
