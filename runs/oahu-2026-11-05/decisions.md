## Shortlist (fragment order)
1. **Waikīkī, Diamond Head end (Kapahulu / Kapiʻolani Park side).**
   - It is the bookable option, because it sits inside the resort zone.
   - Daily life is in reach: Kapiʻolani Park, the Ala Wai and Diamond Head running loops, beaches that are safe in winter, and the W Line from the airport.
   - The Waikiki Shell lot (~$6/day) fits the advice to rent a car for only 1–2 days.
   - The quiet-end claim is a dossier my-pick, labelled as such.
   - The avoid flag (mid-Waikīkī on Kalākaua or Kūhiō) is a real booking trap: those rooms read central on a map and are loud at night (dossier).
2. **Kakaʻako.**
   - It is the strongest daily-life fit on the evidence: Walk Score 94, Whole Foods Queen, two waterfront running parks and a W Line stop.
   - It stays in despite the bookability problem (90-day minimum outside resort zones, a my-pick to verify). That tradeoff is stated plainly in the entry.
3. **Kailua.**
   - It is the one non-Honolulu area that passes the live-there test: a walkable, bikeable town, four supermarkets, and a beach that is swimmable in winter.
   - It also anchors the Plan A split line.

## Split line
- **Plan B (6 nights):** one base, because a split eats too much of a 6-night stay.
- **Plan A (9 nights):** 6 nights in town + 3 in Kailua. This is supported by the dossier's consensus to rent a car for 1–2 days for the North Shore and windward side, and by Kailua being swimmable in winter.
- **North Shore:** a day trip, not a base. Its water is no-swim in winter, Kamehameha Hwy jams on big-swell days and it needs a car.

## Cut list
- **Kaimukī** — quiet low-rise neighbourhood with a strong food strip, but Walk Score is only 58 and there is "very little legal visitor lodging" (dossier my-pick), so the reader can't really book it. It is folded into the Waikīkī entry as a 1.5-mi neighbour instead.
- **Mōʻiliʻili / McCully** — Walk Score 91 and the Ala Wai path, but it is dense, older and student-y. It is also outside the resort zone, so it has the same legal-rental problem as Kakaʻako with less going for it, and it duplicates what Kakaʻako and Waikīkī give.
- **Mānoa** — Walk Score 49, rain most afternoons, scarce parking. The "best for renters" rating is about long-term living, not a week's stay.
- **North Shore (Haleʻiwa)** — fails the test in December. There is no swimming in winter, the single highway jams, the car is essential, buses are slow and Triple Crown brings crowds. It is used in the split line rather than as an entry.
- **Chinatown / downtown** — the dossier has no neighbourhood daily-life data, and its safety note flags visible homelessness. It was not considered further.
- **Ko Olina / Turtle Bay resorts** — not in the dossier. Isolated resort enclaves fail the walkable daily-life test anyway.
