## Economy drivers
- Tourism ~16.1% of GDP, federal ~11.1%, military ~5.7% (2024; BEA/DBEDT/UHERO chart in legislative testimony Jan 2026) — https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/sessions/session2026/testimony/Info_Testimony_WAM_01-07-26_Outlook.pdf ; UHERO's Bonham: tourism + federal ≈ one-third of the economy
- Visitor spending 2025 $21.75B (2024 $20.58B, +5.7%); arrivals 9,642,991 in 2025 vs 9,701,499 in 2024 (-0.6%), still below pre-pandemic peak — DBEDT via Garden Island — https://www.thegardenisland.com/2026/02/02/business/hawaii-visitor-arrivals-end-2025-well-below-pre-pandemic-peak/ ; https://hawaiifreepress.com/Articles-Main/ID/46694/2025-Tourist-Count-Down-Spending-Up
- Military: ranked second only to tourism in GDP contribution per 2025 Military in Hawaiʻi Economic Impact Factbook (dollar figures unreadable in extract, not recorded) — https://npr.brightspotcdn.com/71/e7/8dfe13734aab84fc715e58c09307/military-in-hawaii-economic-impact-factbook-2025-macro-embargoed-2.pdf
- Agriculture past: 1980 had 14 sugar + 4 pineapple plantations on >300,000 acres; by 2017 both crops <5,000 acres; pineapple decline from 1966; Lanai pineapple ended 1992; last sugar (HC&S, Maui) closed 2016 — PBS NewsHour / Spokesman — https://www.pbs.org/newshour/science/paradise-found-hawaiian-agriculture-from-sugar-cane-to-seed-farms ; https://spokesman.com/stories/2017/dec/18/with-pineapple-and-sugar-production-gone-hawaii-we

- Min wage $16/hr from 1 Jan 2026 (via tipping coverage) — https://www.thisweekhawaii.com/hawaii-travel-tips/tipping-guide

## History beats
- Settlement: Polynesian voyagers (Marquesas/Society Islands origin debated); date contested: "short chronology" AD 1000-1200 best supported (Kirch review), others 800-1000 or 1219-1266; only securely dated early site is Bellows Beach (Oahu) — https://researchgate.net/profile/Patrick-Kirch/publication/260248796 ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Hawaii
- Kamehameha I (Hawaiʻi Island chief) united the islands into one kingdom by 1810 (1782-1810 campaigns; Kauai ceded 1810) — https://www.to-hawaii.com/culture/hawaiian-history/ (1795 Nuʻuanu battle not confirmed in search; my knowledge)
- 1820 American Protestant missionaries arrive; sugar plantations begin 1820s-1830s, imported labor (Chinese, Japanese, Portuguese, Filipino) — https://www.to-hawaii.com/culture/hawaiian-history/ ; sugar/plantation history PBS above
- 17 Jan 1893 overthrow of Queen Liliʻuokalani by sugar/pineapple businessmen backed by US troops — https://www.britannica.com/place/Hawaii-state/History ; https://www.history.com/articles/hawaii ; 1993 US apology resolution (own knowledge/search snippet); some Native Hawaiian groups contest annexation legality
- 1898 annexation (McKinley signs Joint Resolution), territory 1900 — https://info.mysticstamp.com/wp-content/uploads/07-07-1898-Hawaii.pdf
- 21 Aug 1959 statehood, 50th state, ~92-93% referendum yes — https://history.com/topics/us-states/hawaii
- Hawaiian Renaissance 1970s (revived hula, voyaging via Hōkūleʻa 1976, Kahoʻolawe protests; Kahoʻolawe returned to state 2003) — sparse in search; Hōkūleʻa/1978 constitution = own knowledge. https://archives.starbulletin.com/2000/03/20/special/story7.html
- Maui wildfire 8 Aug 2023, Lahaina: 102 dead (CBS says 101), ~2,200 structures damaged/destroyed, ~12,000 displaced — https://www.cbsnews.com/news/maui-lahaina-wildfire-victims-reach-4-billion-dollar-settlement ; https://amp.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2024/08/05/325509.htm
- Aftermath: $4.037B global settlement (Aug 2024, ~450 suits; Hawaiian Electric $1.99B, also State, Maui County, Kamehameha Schools, West Maui Land, Hawaiian Telcom, Spectrum) — https://www.hawaiianelectric.com/hawaiian-electric-joins-global-settlement-agreement-with-others-to-resolve-maui-wildfire-litigation ; payment status unconfirmed by primary source (one travel site: 4 annual tranches through 2030)
- Rebuild status: FEMA data cited (Jul 1 2026) 234 homes complete, 310 under construction, 349 permits in process (secondary: hawaiisbesttravel.com — https://hawaiisbesttravel.com/visiting-lahaina-maui-2026/); construction began on 60 Front Street homes 3 Oct 2026 — https://mauinow.com/2026/10/03/construction-begins-on-60-front-street-homes-lost-in-the-lahaina-wildfire/ ; blessing for first commercial rebuild on Front Street 13 Oct 2026 — https://www.hawaiinewsnow.com/2026/10/13/blessing-marks-start-first-commercial-rebuild-lahainas-front-street/

## Culture candidates
- Kapu and sacred sites: kapu signs = off-limits; heiau are living religious sites; stay on paths, don't climb stones, take nothing; no marked boundary = outer edge of structure is the limit; don't stack rocks/wrap in ti leaves (corrupts hoʻokupu offerings) — DLNR Wahi Pana brochure https://dlnr.hawaii.gov/dsp/files/2022/12/Wahi-Pana-brochure-1.pdf ; Lonely Planet https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/things-to-know-traveling-to-hawaii ; https://hawaii-guide.com/hawaii-etiquette-guide
- ʻŌlelo Hawaiʻi revival: ʻAha Pūnana Leo immersion preschools from 1982; ~3,000+ PreK-12 students in 28 public kula kaiapuni (2023); census ~18,610 speakers (older ACS); 26,000 self-identified (2023 statement); NatGeo cites ~100,000 speakers as "safe" benchmark; Honolulu Magazine: ~10,000 native-level, <1% of state — https://www.nationalgeographic.com/culture/article/hawaii-native-language-efforts-to-preserve-revive ; https://uhfoundation.org/saving-hawaiian-language ; https://www.honolulumagazine.com/?p=30151 . Visible: place names with ʻokina/kahakō, "mahalo", "kōkua", "mauka/makai" directions (own knowledge)
- Pidgin (Hawaiʻi Creole English): plantation-era contact language (Hawaiian, English, Portuguese, Cantonese, Japanese, Filipino); Census recognized it 2015 but counted only ~1,600 speakers; Ethnologue ~600,000 L1 — https://www.nbcnews.com/news/asian-america/hawaiian-language-pidgin-data-revealed-new-u-s-census-bureau-n495006 ; https://hlt.bme.hu/en/dld/language/7211
- Aloha and kuleana: searches returned nothing substantive on kuleana or Mālama Hawaiʻi; aloha-as-ethic, kuleana = responsibility/stewardship, plate-lunch/potluck/"local style" cross-ethnic mix are own knowledge, unsourced
- Reef and ocean: chemical sunscreens with oxybenzone/octinoxate banned statewide (since Jan 2021, own knowledge); mineral sunscreen at Hanauma Bay etc. — https://disneyblog.com/guide/oahu/hawaiian-culture/ ; ocean safety (shore break, currents, big winter North Shore surf Nov-Mar, lifeguard flags) not sourced by search — own knowledge
- Tipping: Toast Q2 2026 avg full-service tip in Hawaii 18.8% (US 19.1%); guides: 20% standard at resort areas, 15% reads as weak, auto-gratuity 18-20% for large parties common, $1/drink beer-wine, 20% cocktails, valet $2-5; guides vary 15-25% — https://www.hawaii-guide.com/blog/tipping-in-hawaii-visitor-guide ; https://www.thisweekhawaii.com/hawaii-travel-tips/tipping-guide ; https://beatofhawaii.com/hawaii-restaurants-suggest-25-tips-diners-are-paying-less/ . Service charges/resort fees: SB2699 2024 on fees https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/sessions/session2024/bills/SB2699_.pdf

## Languages
- English and Hawaiian both official state languages (own knowledge); English universal for visitors. Hawaiian: see above. Pidgin heard locally; Census-listed other languages (Ilocano, Tagalog, Japanese) per language sources — https://teachling.wwu.edu/language-hawaii . English-only travelers have no barrier; Pidgin/Hawaiian words appear on signs and menus.
- Phrases (Hawaiian, for the drafter; own knowledge, unsourced): mahalo (thanks), aloha (hello/goodbye/love), E ʻoluʻolu (please), kala mai (sorry/excuse me), ʻae (yes), ʻaʻole (no), lua/halepaku (bathroom—"lua" common), ʻono (delicious).
