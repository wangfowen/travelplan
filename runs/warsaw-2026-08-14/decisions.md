## Revision 4 — the 小红书 vision pass

### MEI — tag stacked, and the entry rewritten around the new detail
Image 6/14 is captioned **MEI**. 小红书's top Korean pick at 6/6 ("韩餐天花板") and Reddit's most-named Korean BBQ are one restaurant. `reddit` + `小红书` stacked.

The description changed from a one-dish line to what the entry style actually asks for: **order the fried chicken and the tteokbokki, skip the bibimbap** (mediocre and not served in a stone bowl). A named dish to avoid, from a reviewer who rated the place 6/6, is a harder and more useful fact than another recommendation — and it is only obtainable from a first-hand local-language source. Pajeon, kimchi jeon and samgyupsal recorded as the rest of the table. Bulgogi kept: it is Reddit's named order and does not conflict.

*One discrepancy left visible rather than smoothed:* the 小红书 text describes the 6/6 Korean place as far from the centre, while MEI is on Kruczkowskiego in Powiśle, which is central. Most likely the author lives outside the centre; possibly it has more than one site. ### Izumi Sushi — CUT
Newly identified as the 4/6 sushi place. Verified at **4.6 ★ (5,632), Biały Kamień 4**, Maps "Authentic Japanese", open to 11pm. The rating is genuinely strong and 5,632 reviews is a real base — under food.md, "a rating that holds up against the city's best" is listed as qualifying evidence, so it was properly considered rather than dismissed.

**Cut, for three reasons, none of which is distance:**
1. **The verdict is lukewarm.** 4/6 and 不踩雷 — "a safe bet", "you won't go wrong". That is the identical phrase and the identical score on which I cut 兴隆小吃 in revision 1. Cutting one and keeping the other would be incoherent.
2. **The food evidence is freshness plus a workaround.** What the source actually says is that the salmon and ingredients are fresh, and that the house dipping sauce is too cloying so you should ask for the mild one. A tip for making a dish less unpleasant is not evidence the kitchen is good.

Explicitly **not** cut for being at Biały Kamień — food.md states that distance from the centre is never a strike and that an outlying district is not a reason to drop a candidate, and that rule is respected here (Targowisko Bakalarska, further out, is in).

## The three source conflicts the dossier flagged

### Pańska 85 — CUT
Evidence for: one Reddit commenter naming it in thread [D]; the task brief also reports local-press support (no local-press mention of it appears in `food-editorial.md`, so that leg is unverifiable from my tracks).
Evidence against: a Chinese-speaking Warsaw resident who rates Chinese restaurants across the city puts it at **3/6** — roast duck acceptable, everything else 很一般, portions small, and the 肉夹馍 called a fake ("完全不是酥皮，只是馒头夹肉").
**Ruling: out.** food.md's rule on local complaints is to read *which* complaint it is — "touristy" or "overhyped" are not reasons to cut, "the food is bad" is. This is the second kind, and it is the most qualified voice in the pool on this specific cuisine: a resident eating Chinese food constantly, rating on a scale where they hand out 5s and 6s to others. A single Reddit naming does not outweigh a specific dish-by-dish negative from the person best placed to judge. Two better-evidenced Chinese options exist in the same track (哈尔滨饺子馆 5/6, 兴隆小吃 4/6), so cutting it costs nothing in coverage.

### GOŚCINIEC Polskie Pierogi — IN, with the order reversed

Three independent 小红书 mentions (posts 2 and 4 plus a comment) all say the same thing: the **flaki** is the reason to go, the pierogi are 不踩雷但一般. ## The ticket-payment conflict — how it was resolved
Both claims are true about different acts, and the section says so explicitly in one place:

The SEO claim of a live "Open Payment" with a 15 PLN daily cap in Warsaw is named as wrong rather than silently omitted, because a reader who has seen it will otherwise board expecting to tap.
- **Live:** buying a ticket from the machine with a contactless card. Confirmed from official-domain sources (ztm.waw.pl / wtp.waw.pl indexed pages): **onboard bus and tram machines are card-only, no cash**; stationary machines take cards, cash and BLIK. `wtp.waw.pl` and `ztm.waw.pl` both return 403 to direct fetch, so this is snippet-level confirmation of official pages, not an aggregator.
