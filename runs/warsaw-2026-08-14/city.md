## 1. Atlas Obscura — Warsaw

Filtered to the genuinely odd; the big-name museums Atlas Obscura also lists (POLIN, Uprising Museum, Copernicus Science Centre) are deliberately not repeated here.

- **Keret House (Dom Kereta)** — the world's narrowest house, a ~4-foot-wide insertable dwelling wedged between two buildings, ul. Żelazna/Chłodna, Wola — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/keret-house
- **Skarpa Ski Jump** — ruins of a late-1950s ski-jump ramp still standing on the escarpment inside the city — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/skarpa-ski-jump
- **Galeria Forty / Forty** — art gallery hidden inside an abandoned 19th-c. Warsaw fort — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/galeria-forty-forty
- **"A Footbridge of Memory" (Kładka Pamięci), ul. Chłodna** — 2011 monument by Tomasz Tusch-Lec that lights a ghostly outline at night of the wooden bridge that joined the Great Ghetto to the Small Ghetto — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/footbridge-of-memory-warsaw-poland
- **Monument Tree of Pawiak** — bronze cast of the elm that survived the 1944 destruction of Pawiak prison and became the spot where families nailed obituary plaques; the real tree died and was replaced with the bronze copy — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/the-monument-tree-of-pawiak
- **Underground library in a suburban metro station** — Space-Age-styled library inside an M1 station, with its own hydroponic garden — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland (station name not confirmed in the snippet; verify before printing)
- **Metro tile installations by a named Polish artist** — complex ceramic-tile works commissioned inside the Warsaw subway — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland/subways (artist name not recovered; verify)
- **Neon Museum (Muzeum Neonów), Soho Factory, Praga Południe** — rescued communist-era neon signage; photography allowed with phones only, no DSLRs — `atlas obscura` `in your pocket` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/the-neon-museum-warsaw-poland
- **Poster Museum / communist-era music-poster collection (Wilanów)** — national collection of Communist-era musical posters and post-Stalin "Communist glamour" graphics — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland/museums
- **Museum of Life under Communism (Muzeum Życia w PRL)** — hidden collection of ordinary objects illustrating daily life in communist Poland — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland/museums
- **National Museum of Technology, Palace of Culture and Science** — 1st floor of the PKiN; holds the first Enigma machine built in Poland, plus Polish computers, cameras, motorcycles and cars — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/the-museum-of-technology-warsaw-poland
- **Canaletto Room, Royal Castle** — the 20+ camera-obscura-accurate Bernardo Bellotto cityscapes (1770s) that were used as the working blueprints for rebuilding the Old Town after 1945 — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/canaletto-room-royal-palace
- **Chopin's heart, Holy Cross Church (Krakowskie Przedmieście)** — the composer's sister smuggled his heart out of Paris to be interred in a pillar of the church — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland/monuments
- **Tchorek plaques** — standardised stone plaques across the city marking sites of German executions and battles during the occupation; they are a city-wide walkable set, not a single site — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland/monuments
- **Copernicus monument and its model solar system, Krakowskie Przedmieście** — statue with an accompanying scale solar system laid into the ground — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland/monuments
- **Powązki Cemetery** — one of Europe's largest cemeteries, 200,000+ burials; also the site of the official 14 Aug 2026 Armed Forces Day remembrance ceremony (see §5) — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland
- **St. John's Archcathedral / royal reburial** — the last king of Poland was interred here nearly 200 years after his death — `atlas obscura` — https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland/architecture
- Atlas Obscura's Warsaw hub counts **47 entries** as of Aug 2026 — https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland ; full list at https://www.atlasobscura.com/things-to-do/warsaw-poland/places

## 3. Neighbourhoods — daily-life facts

For the "could I live here for a week" test: quiet, walkable, daily life in reach. Nightlife and proximity to sights are recorded only as noise/liveability facts, not as merits.

### Śródmieście (central district, both the core and Powiśle sit inside it administratively)
- Transit: both metro lines and their only interchange — **M1 × M2 at Świętokrzyska**, plus Centrum, Ratusz Arsenał, Politechnika, Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet, Rondo ONZ; essentially every tram trunk route passes through — `my pick` (metro station geography), corroborated https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M1_(Warsaw)
- Bar/noise strips, named: **ul. Poznańska, ul. Żurawia, ul. Nowogrodzka, and around plac Zbawiciela** carry the concentration of restaurants, bars and clubs — `rynekpierwotny` — https://rynekpierwotny.pl/wiadomosci-mieszkaniowe/warszawskie-srodmiescie-dlaczego-warto-tu-zamieszkac/12085/
- Plac Zbawiciela sits where Marszałkowska, Mokotowska, Nowowiejska and al. Wyzwolenia meet; described as a busy meeting place ringed with food and drink venues — `srodmiescie.um.warszawa.pl` — https://srodmiescie.um.warszawa.pl/-/plac-zbawiciela
- Named drawbacks of living here: **noise, traffic and air quality**; residents around Nowy Świat/Śródmieście have campaigned for years over party noise — `rynekpierwotny` — https://rynekpierwotny.pl/wiadomosci-mieszkaniowe/warszawskie-srodmiescie-dlaczego-warto-tu-zamieszkac/12085/
- Green/running in reach: **20 parks in the district**, incl. Łazienki Królewskie, Ogród Saski, Park Ujazdowski, Ogród Krasińskich — `rynekpierwotny` — same link
- Food hall: **Hala Gwardii** (world cuisines) named as the district's food hall — `rynekpierwotny` — same link

### Powiśle (riverside strip of Śródmieście)
- Transit: **M2 station Centrum Nauki Kopernik** sits in Powiśle; Nowy Świat-Uniwersytet (M2) is the walk-up alternative; **PKP/SKM Warszawa Powiśle** station gives SKM + Koleje Mazowieckie; trams and buses on the Wisłostrada/Solec corridors — `hamilton may` — https://www.hamiltonmay.com/area-guide/powisle ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centrum_Nauki_Kopernik_metro_station
- Character: National Museum, Copernicus Science Centre, the Vistula boulevards and the university campus are all walkable, and **the district goes quiet in the evening** — `hamilton may` — https://www.hamiltonmay.pl/miejski-przewodnik/powisle
- Counter-fact on quiet: **expect noise and heavy traffic on Friday and Saturday** — `hamilton may` — same link
- Running: the Vistula Boulevards are several km of continuous paved riverside path directly off Powiśle — the single best running access of any central neighbourhood — `great runs` `in your pocket` — https://greatruns.com/warsaw-vistula-river-paths/
- Summer overlay: seasonal shipping-container bars, floating barges and food trucks along the boulevards — `in your pocket` — https://www.inyourpocket.com/warsaw/powisle
- **Live caveat for these dates:** Wisłostrada — the road along the Powiśle riverfront — is closed 16:00 Fri 14 Aug to 16:00 Sat 15 Aug 2026 for the military parade (see §5)

### Praga Północ (right bank)
- Transit: **two M2 stations, Dworzec Wileński and Szwedzka**, putting the centre under 10 minutes away; dense tram network on **ul. Targowa and al. Solidarności** — `praga-pn.waw.pl` — https://praga-pn.waw.pl/
- Bar/character strips: **ul. Ząbkowska, ul. Brzeska, ul. Targowa** — rows of surviving pre-war tenements, cobbles and courtyards; Ząbkowska is the bar/street-art strip — `in your pocket` `praga-pn.waw.pl` — https://www.inyourpocket.com/warsaw/praga-warsaws-alternative-right-bank_73743f
- Daily shopping: **Bazar Różyckiego**, ul. Targowa 54 next to Dworzec Wileński metro — the biggest bazaar in communist Poland, **now largely empty** (negative evidence: do not plan groceries around it) — `in your pocket` — same link
- Green/running: **Park Praski**, the municipal Zoo, and the wilder right-bank Vistula beaches — the right bank is the undeveloped, sandy side of the river — `praga-pn.waw.pl` — https://praga-pn.waw.pl/
- Live-here texture: courtyard-centred, multi-generational neighbourhood bonds; the district trades on preserved historic fabric rather than amenity density — `praga-pn.waw.pl` — same link
- Summer overlay: **Lato Konesera** open-air programme at Centrum Praskie Koneser runs through August — a nightly draw inside the district — `warsaw insider` — https://warsawinsider.pl/where-contemporary-warsaw-spends-its-time/

### Praga Południe / Saska Kępa
- Transit, Saska Kępa specifically: **no metro in the neighbourhood**; nearest are **Stadion Narodowy (M2), ~2.2 km**, and Politechnika/Centrum across the river — `moovit stop data` — https://moovitapp.com/index/en/public_transit-Saska_K%C4%99pa-Warsaw-site_19232720-1062
- **Rondo Waszyngtona is the transit hub** for the area: trams **7, 8, 9, 22, 24, 25**; buses 102, 111, 117, 123, 138, 146, 147, 158, 166; express 507, 509, 517, 521; night N02, N22, N24, N72 — `saskakepa.info` — https://saskakepa.info/o-kepie/ruch-na-kepie/komunikacja-na-saskiej-kepie-autobusy-tramwaje/
- Tram geography: trams run to the centre over **Poniatowski Bridge**, to Grochów along **al. Waszyngtona**, and to Praga along **al. Zieleniecka** — `saskakepa.info` — same link
- Interior streets of Saska Kępa are served by **buses only** (e.g. Meksykańska: 117, 146; ul. Saska: 141, 143, 151, 182, 188, 411, 502, 514, 520, 523, 525, N25) — a 5–12 min walk to the nearest tram — `moovit stop data` — https://moovitapp.com/index/en/public_transit-Saska_K%C4%99pa-Warsaw-site_19232720-1062
- Bar/café strip: **ul. Francuska** (and ul. Paryska) — the whole of Saska Kępa's evening activity is on those two streets; the rest is villa-quiet — `in your pocket` — https://www.inyourpocket.com/warsaw/Saska-Kepa_73744f
- Supermarket access: neighbourhood grocery ~190 m and **Biedronka ~800 m** from central Saska Kępa addresses; **Park Skaryszewski ~1.2 km** — `moovit / listing data` — https://moovitapp.com/index/en/public_transit-Saska_K%C4%99pa-Warsaw-site_19232720-1062
- Running: **Park Skaryszewski**, 58 ha, lake, rose garden, amphitheatre, paved loops — the running park of the right bank — `great runs` — https://greatruns.com/warsaw-skaryszewski-park-and-central-stadium/
- Praga Południe more broadly (Grochów, Kamionek): arteries **ul. Grochowska, al. Waszyngtona, al. Stanów Zjednoczonych, ul. Ostrobramska**; **rondo Wiatraczna** is the transport heart of Grochów; tram lines **3, 6, 9, 22** run the Grochowska corridor — `grochowniepragapd` `tvn warszawa` — https://tvn24.pl/tvnwarszawa/praga-poludnie/warszawa-od-poniedzialku-grochowska-bez-tramwajow-st9040103
- Warning riding on Grochowska: the corridor took a full tram closure in **May 2026** for switch and rail replacement at the Gocławek loop (lines 3, 6, 9 cut back to rondo Wiatraczna; 22 rerouted; replaced by buses 123, 125, 141, 142, 143, 145, 148, 173, 183, 521, 702, 704, 720, 722, 730, N03, N24) — that work is finished, but the corridor is under active renewal — `tvn warszawa`, published 10 May 2026 — same link
- Expat framing: Praga-Południe is the gentrifying right bank — cheaper than the central districts, growing amenities, valued by families — `expat arrivals` — https://www.expatarrivals.com/europe/poland/warsaw/areas-and-suburbs-warsaw

### Żoliborz
- Transit: **M1 runs the length of the district** — Dworzec Gdański, Plac Wilsona and Marymont are the Żoliborz stations (Słodowiec/Stare Bielany are just north in Bielany). Plac Wilsona station sits at Słowackiego/Krasińskiego — `pl.wikipedia` — https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plac_Wilsona_(stacja_metra)
- Trams run on **ul. Słowackiego, ul. Mickiewicza, ul. Popiełuszki and al. Jana Pawła II** — `gethome` — https://gethome.pl/blog/dzielnica-zoliborz/
- Character: one of the greenest districts, a dense cluster of pre-war villas (the other such cluster is Saska Kępa), small parks, cafés and family services; explicitly recommended for **quiet focus** — `expat arrivals` `hamilton may` — https://www.expatarrivals.com/europe/poland/warsaw/areas-and-suburbs-warsaw
- Weekend food market at **Skwer Śmiałej** — `expat arrivals` — same link
- Running: **Park Kępa Potocka**, ~18 ha of paths along the Potok channel, plus the Vistula path and **Las Bielański** (Bielany Forest) immediately north; the Olympic Centre sits beside Kępa Potocka — `great runs` `gethome` — https://greatruns.com/location/warsaw-poland/
- Riverside: **Żoliborz Beach** near the Warsaw Citadel — `in your pocket` — https://www.inyourpocket.com/warsaw/praga-district-beach_154815v
- Rent scale for reference: 3-bed PLN 7,000–12,000/month + utilities (2026) — `expat arrivals` — https://www.expatarrivals.com/europe/poland/warsaw/areas-and-suburbs-warsaw

### Mokotów
- Transit: **four M1 stations in the district — Pole Mokotowskie, Racławicka, Wierzbno, Wilanowska** (plus Służew/Ursynów further south); Warszawa Mokotów and Warszawa Służewiec rail stations serve the SKM/KM lines — `en.wikipedia` — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pole_Mokotowskie_metro_station ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilanowska_metro_station
- The **Metro Pole Mokotowskie** interchange (al. Niepodległości × ul. Rakowiecka, in Stary Mokotów) combines M1 with trams **17, 33**, rail R1/R3/S4, and buses 128, 136, 143, 167, 174, 175, 182, 186, 187, 188, 504, 523, N32, N34 — `warszawikia` — https://warszawa.fandom.com/wiki/Zesp%C3%B3%C5%82_przystankowy_Metro_Pole_Mokotowskie
- **New in Dec 2025:** a tram route opened along **ul. Rakowiecka**; three bus stops at Pole Mokotowskie were removed and replaced by two new tram stops — `warszawikia` — same link
- Running: **Pole Mokotowskie**, 70 ha, paved and dirt paths — the biggest in-city running park on the left bank, on the Mokotów/Ochota boundary; **Park Morskie Oko** and Łazienki are the other loops — `great runs` — https://greatruns.com/warsaw-mokotowskie-field/
- Character: consistently the expat default — close to centre, described as having exceptional transport links; the southern corridor Mokotów→Wilanów→Konstancin is the historic expat heartland and where the international schools are — `expat arrivals` — https://www.expatarrivals.com/europe/poland/warsaw/areas-and-suburbs-warsaw
- Rent scale: 3-bed in central Mokotów PLN 9,000–16,000/month + utilities (2026) — the most expensive of the districts surveyed — `expat arrivals` — same link

### Wola
- Transit: **five M2 stations — Rondo Daszyńskiego, Płocka, Młynów, Księcia Janusza, Ulrychów** (Księcia Janusza, Młynów, Płocka opened 2020; Ulrychów and Bemowo opened 30 Jun 2022) — `en.wikipedia` — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C5%82yn%C3%B3w_metro_station
- Trams run on **ul. Wolska** (new route feeding M2 at Rondo Daszyńskiego and Rondo ONZ, and the Warszawa Wola rail station), **ul. Górczewska** and **ul. Okopowa/al. Jana Pawła II** — `um.warszawa.pl` — https://um.warszawa.pl/-/tramwaje-pojechaly-wolska
- Named "one of Warsaw's best-connected districts" — `gethome` — https://gethome.pl/blog/dzielnica-wola/
- Running / recreation: **Park Moczydło** (swimming pools, water playground, slides, artificial river) and **Park Edwarda Szymańskiego**, both ~300 m west of Młynów M2 station; **Park Sowińskiego** further west — `en.wikipedia` `gethome` — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C5%82yn%C3%B3w_metro_station
- Texture: Wola is the office/high-rise growth district around Rondo Daszyńskiego, with older low-rise fabric west of Młynów — `gethome` — https://gethome.pl/blog/dzielnica-wola/

### Ochota
- Transit: **no metro station inside Ochota proper** — Pole Mokotowskie (M1) sits on the boundary, Politechnika and Centrum are the other walk-ups. Trams **1, 7, 9, 14, 15, 25** run the **ul. Grójecka** and **al. Jerozolimskie** corridors; a tram depot on ul. Banacha is served by lines **1, 14, 15** — `zyciewarszawy` `gethome` — https://gethome.pl/blog/dzielnica-ochota/
- **Grójecka is the road to Chopin Airport**; a new tram link between Warszawa Zachodnia station and Grójecka is under construction — `gethome` — same link
- Quiet fabric: **Kolonia Staszica** (between ul. Wawelska and ul. Filtrowa, begun in the early 1920s) and **Kolonie Lubeckiego** — low pre-war buildings and green squares; the rest of the district is PRL-era estates (Rakowiec, Szczęśliwice) and offices along al. Jerozolimskie — `zyciewarszawy` — https://zyciewarszawy.pl/sprawy-urzedowe/art41040301-dzielnice-warszawy-ochota-lokalizacja-liczba-mieszkancow-komunikacja-atrakcje
- Daily shopping: **Hala Banacha** market hall (ul. Banacha) — the district's fresh-food market — `zyciewarszawy` — same link
- Running: **Pole Mokotowskie** connects to Ochota via ul. Banacha to the Żwirki i Wigury junction; **Park Szczęśliwicki** with the artificial hill is the western option — `zyciewarszawy` `great runs` — same link

## 4. Transit and practical mechanics

### Modes and system
- Operator: **ZTM Warszawa**, branded **Warszawski Transport Publiczny (WTP)** — one fare system spans **metro (M1, M2), trams, buses, night buses, and SKM city trains**; Koleje Mazowieckie (KM) regional trains are included within the ZTM zones on a common ticket — `ztm/wtp` — https://www.wtp.waw.pl/en/ticket-tariff/
- **Zones 1 and 2.** Zone 1 covers the city; zone 2 the surrounding communes. Both airports and everything a visitor needs are effectively zone-1 business except Modlin — `ztm/wtp` — https://www.wtp.waw.pl/en/maps-schemas/ticket-zone-boundaries/
- **M1** runs north–south, Młociny (Bielany) → Kabaty (Ursynów), through Żoliborz, Śródmieście and Mokotów. **M2** runs east–west, Bemowo (Wola) → Bródno (Targówek), through Wola, Śródmieście and Praga Północ. **They cross only at Świętokrzyska** — `en.wikipedia` — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Metro
- M2's final three Bemowo stations — **Lazurowa, Chrzanów, Karolin** — plus Karolin depot were under construction with **opening planned for 2026**; not confirmed open as of Aug 2026 — **unconfirmed** — `en.wikipedia` — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M2_(Warsaw)
- Metro operating hours reported as roughly **04:55 to ~00:30 Mon–Thu, ~02:30 Fri–Sat, ~23:30 Sun**, with ZM1/ZM2 replacement buses overnight. Only found on an unofficial tracker (metrowawa.pl), **not confirmed against ZTM** — **unconfirmed** — https://metrowawa.pl/en/godziny
- **Night buses** run as N-prefixed lines (N02, N22, N24, N25, N32, N34, N72 appear in the district data above) on the same ZTM fare — `saskakepa.info` — https://saskakepa.info/o-kepie/ruch-na-kepie/komunikacja-na-saskiej-kepie-autobusy-tramwaje/

### Payment mechanics — the key point
- **You cannot yet tap a contactless bank card on a bus or tram as your ticket.** Warsaw's open-payment system, the **Warszawski System Biletowy (WSB)**, had its contract signed with **Mennica Polska on 19 Sep 2025**, requires ~13,000 new on-board validators plus a new passenger portal, app, central system and journey planner, and is scheduled for **2027** — `um.warszawa.pl` — https://um.warszawa.pl/-/nowy-system-biletowy ; https://um.warszawa.pl/-/warszawski-system-biletowy-coraz-blizej
- What WSB will eventually do (2027, not now): tap the card/phone/watch to the validator on boarding = buy + validate in one motion, with automatic settlement calculating the cheapest fare, **capped at the 24-hour ticket price** — `um.warszawa.pl` — same links
- **Several SEO travel sites assert that "Open Payment" tap-to-ride with a PLN 15/day and PLN 180/30-day cap is live in Warsaw in 2026. That contradicts the city's own WSB timeline and should be treated as false unless verified on the ground.** Recorded here specifically so the brief does not repeat it — `negative evidence` — https://expresstransit.com/warsaw/ (claim) vs https://um.warszawa.pl/-/nowy-system-biletowy (city)
- **What actually works today:** contactless card / Apple Pay / Google Pay / Samsung Pay at **ticket machines** (station machines and the machines on board buses and trams) to *buy* a ticket, which you then **validate** in the validator. Contactless BLIK is also accepted at stationary and mobile validators — `mennica polska` — https://en.mennica.com.pl/about-us/news/news-details/mint-of-poland-now-offers-contactless-blik-as-payment-method-for-public-transport-ticket-purchase
- **Apps that sell ZTM tickets:** moBiLET, mPay, SkyCash, zBiletem, **jakdojade.pl**, GoPay. App tickets must still be validated after boarding, or before the metro gateline — `warszawa19115` — https://warszawa19115.pl/en/-/zakup-biletu-ztm-w-aplikacjach-mobilnych
- **jakdojade** — the app to actually install: real-time journey planning across all Warsaw modes plus rail and long-distance bus, and it sells the ticket in the same app — `warszawa19115` `imako` — https://warszawa19115.pl/en/-/zakup-biletu-ztm-w-aplikacjach-mobilnych
- **mobiWAWA** — the *official* City of Warsaw app. Note: it is **mobiWAWA, not "mobiWAY"**. It covers **30-day and 90-day tickets only** (normal and reduced, zones 1 / 2 / 1+2, plus the Warsaw Resident Ticket and the 65+ annual) — **useless for a 4-day trip** — `ztm/wtp` — https://www.wtp.waw.pl/gdzie-kupic-bilet/aplikacja-mobiwawa/
- **koleo.pl** — Polish rail ticketing across operators: PKP Intercity, Koleje Mazowieckie, PKP SKM, POLREGIO and the regional carriers, plus FlixBus/Leo Express/RegioJet. This is the app for Modlin trains and any intercity day trip, **not** for city transit — `koleo` — https://koleo.pl/en
- Where to buy without an app: ZTM ticket machines at metro stations and on board buses/trams, ZTM Passenger Service Points (POP), kiosks — `ztm/wtp` — https://www.wtp.waw.pl/en/where-to-buy-a-ticket/ ; https://www.wtp.waw.pl/en/where-to-buy-a-ticket/ticket-machines/

## 5. August specifics — the 15 August public holiday

**15 Aug 2026 falls on a Saturday.** It is a double holiday: **Wniebowzięcie NMP (Assumption)** and **Święto Wojska Polskiego (Polish Armed Forces Day)**. It is a statutory non-working day. This lands squarely mid-trip.

### The parade and street closures
- **Military parade on Wisłostrada, starting 12:00, Sat 15 Aug 2026** — thousands of troops, tanks, helicopters, F-35s, allied contingents; theme "Bezpieczna Polska"; marks the **106th anniversary of the 1920 Battle of Warsaw**. A simultaneous naval parade runs in Gdańsk Bay — `warszawa19115` — https://warszawa19115.pl/-/swieto-wojska-polskiego-2026
- **Best public viewing point per the city: the slope at the Multimedia Fountain Park, near ul. Sanguszki and Wisłostrada**, easiest reached from the Old Town — `warszawa19115` — same link
- **Road closures: 16:00 Fri 14 Aug → 16:00 Sat 15 Aug 2026.** Both carriageways of **Wisłostrada** from the Skłodowska-Curie Bridge to the Łazienkowski Bridge, plus the Wilanów-bound lane onward to ul. Gagarina. Also closed: **Krasińskiego, Krajewskiego, Wenedów, Sanguszki, Karowa, Lipowa, Dobra, Tamka, Ludna, Górnośląska, Wilanowska** — i.e. every street that drops from Śródmieście down to the river through Powiśle — `rp.pl` `rdc` — https://www.rp.pl/kraj/art44980601-defilada-w-warszawie-utrudnienia-w-ruchu-od-14-sierpnia-zamkniete-ulice-i-zmiany-w-komunikacji-miejskiej
- **Pedestrian and cycle closures 11:00–14:00 Sat 15 Aug:** the **Gdański Bridge and Śląsko-Dąbrowski Bridge** shut to pedestrians and cyclists — the two main walking crossings between the Old Town side and Praga — `warszawa19115` — https://warszawa19115.pl/-/swieto-wojska-polskiego-2026
- **Transit reroutes, same window (16:00 Fri → 16:00 Sat):** buses **100, 106, 107, 108, 114, 116, 118, 127, 128, 131, 141, 159, 162, 166, 167, 175, 178, 180, 185, 187, 190, 197, 409, 500, 503** and night lines; trams **1, 4, 6, 18, 20, 28, 76, T** — `rp.pl` — https://www.rp.pl/kraj/art44980601-defilada-w-warszawie-utrudnienia-w-ruchu-od-14-sierpnia-zamkniete-ulice-i-zmiany-w-komunikacji-miejskiej
- Related ceremonies: **13 Aug** medal ceremony at the Battle of Warsaw 1920 Museum in Ossów; **14 Aug 19:00** remembrance ceremony at **Powązki Cemetery**; **15 Aug** military mass and wreath-layings before the parade; **16 Aug 14:30** battle reconstruction at Ossów — `warszawa19115` — https://warszawa19115.pl/-/swieto-wojska-polskiego-2026

### What a Polish public holiday does to schedules
- **ZTM runs the holiday (Sunday) timetable on 15 August** — day buses, trams and metro on the holiday schedule, night buses on the weekend schedule; individual lines get suspended and the metro can run a special schedule. This is the standard pattern for Polish statutory holidays — `um.warszawa.pl` — https://um.warszawa.pl/-/dlugi-weekend-sierpniowy-w-komunikacji-miejskiej ; the city maintains a standing list of weekdays that run holiday timetables — https://warszawa19115.pl/en/-/dni-powszednie-w-ktorych-obowiazuje-swiateczny-rozklad-jazdy-komunikacji-miejskiej
- **Shops: closed two days running, 15 and 16 August 2026.** 15 Aug is a statutory holiday under the trading ban; **16 Aug is an ordinary non-trading Sunday** (the next trading Sunday is **30 Aug 2026**). **Biedronka, Lidl, Kaufland, Aldi, Netto, Dino, Carrefour, Auchan and the shopping malls are all shut both days** — `forsal` — https://forsal.pl/gospodarka/aktualnosci/artykuly/11286207,sklepy-otwarte-15-i-16-sierpnia-2026-r-lista-sklepow.html
- **The exception:** the trading ban does not cover shops where the owner personally serves customers. Some **Żabka** franchises use this exemption — but not all of them, and not always for full hours — `forsal` — same link. **Practical consequence: buy groceries Thu 13 or Fri 14 August.** Restaurants and cafés are not covered by the ban and open normally.
- **Museums split, they do not follow one rule.** Confirmed for 15 Aug: **Muzeum Historii Polski open 10:00–19:00 with free entry** (https://muzhp.pl/wydarzenia/swieto-wojska-polskiego-w-muzeum-historii-polski); **POLIN closed** (it closes on selected state and religious holidays); **Muzeum Żołnierzy Wyklętych, ul. Rakowiecka 37, closed** (https://rakowiecka37.pl/komunikaty/15-sierpnia-muzeum-bedzie-nieczynne/). **Warsaw Uprising Museum and Łazienki hours for 15 Aug not confirmed — check each venue individually.**
- Assumption is also a major church feast: expect masses, processions and pilgrimage traffic across the city on 15 Aug — `my pick`
