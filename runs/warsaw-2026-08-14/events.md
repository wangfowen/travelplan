### What closes / changes on Sat 15 Aug — and Sun 16 Aug

- **SHOPS SHUT TWO DAYS RUNNING.** 15 Aug is a statutory holiday, and Poland's trading ban covers holidays as well as Sundays; **16 Aug is a non-trading Sunday**. Supermarkets, hypermarkets, discounters and most mall units are closed **both Sat 15 and Sun 16**. Exempt: small owner-operated shops, petrol stations, pharmacies, (in practice) Żabka-type convenience. **The only trading Sunday in Aug 2026 is 30 Aug.** — `local-press` — https://www.gazetaprawna.pl/biznes/finanse-i-gospodarka/artykuly/11286826,dwa-dni-bez-zakupow-sklepy-beda-zamkniete-przez-caly-weekend.html and https://kadry.infor.pl/kadry/indywidualne_prawo_pracy/czas_pracy/7500854,czy-16-sierpnia-to-niedziela-handlowa-niedziele-handlowe-w-2026-roku.html
