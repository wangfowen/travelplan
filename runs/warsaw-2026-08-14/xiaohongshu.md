## Food — Chinese / Korean / Japanese / Vietnamese

Post 3 is a resident's rated roundup. Their star scale runs to 6.

- **中国人家** — Chinese; rated 6/6 "中餐天花板" (the ceiling of Chinese food here), eaten many times, owner and staff friendly — 小红书 [post 3] — **Polish/Latin name not given in post text; it is in the image carousel** (unretrieved)
- **哈尔滨饺子馆 (Harbin Dumpling House)** — Chinese/northeastern; 5/6, eaten many times; specialties 炸串 (fried skewers), 豆皮卷香菜 (tofu-skin roll with coriander), 烤馒头 (grilled mantou); dumplings and stir-fries both — 小红书 [post 3]
- **兴隆小吃 (兴隆酒店分店)** — Chinese; 4/6, "不踩雷" (safe bet); biángbiáng noodles are the most-ordered dish; left door = lunchbox/canteen counter, right door = sit-down restaurant — 小红书 [post 3]
- **Pańska 85** — Chinese; **3/6 — negative evidence.** Roast duck acceptable, everything else "很一般" (very average), portions small; the 肉夹馍 is called a miss — "完全不是酥皮，只是馒头夹肉" (not crisp pastry at all, just meat in a steamed bun) — 小红书 [post 3]. *Note: this place is recommended by localpress and Reddit in other tracks; this is the only Chinese-language assessment of it.*
- **Korean, name in image only** — rated 6/6, "韩餐天花板"; fried chicken and 年糕 (tteokbokki) singled out; **bibimbap not recommended** (not a stone bowl); location is far from centre; author wishes they'd open a branch — 小红书 [post 3] — name not in post text
- **home-BBQ** — Korean BBQ; 3/6, "有点小贵" (a bit pricey); located in **越南村** ("Vietnamese village"), next door to a Chongqing hotpot place — 小红书 [post 3]
- **ukiuki (Uki)** — Japanese ramen; 4/6, now three branches, always a queue; author's preference **miso** over the classic (dislikes the pork note); cold noodles summer-only; always orders matcha ice cream + glutinous rice balls — 小红书 [post 3]
- **Arigator** — Japanese ramen; 3/6, long queues, "many say it's good but I prefer Uki" — 小红书 [post 3]
- **Sushi, name in image only** — 4/6, salmon and ingredients fresh; **ask for the sauce łagodne (mild)** — the pictured mayo-style sauce with fried items is cloying — 小红书 [post 3]
- **BIN BIN** — Vietnamese; 3/6 — 小红书 [post 3]
- **越南村 (Vietnamese quarter)** — a district, not a venue: contains Chongqing hotpot, home-BBQ Korean and more — 小红书 [post 3] — *corroborates the Bakalarska Asian food cluster named repeatedly in reddit.md*

## Food — Polish and other

- **GOŚCINIEC Polskie Pierogi** — **named 3× across posts 2, 4 and the comments.** The reason to go is the **flaki (牛肚汤, tripe soup)**, not the pierogi: post 4's author (who states they avoid influencer restaurants — "旅行几乎不去网红店") calls it the best tripe soup they've had anywhere and says it is mildly spicy; the potato pancakes are also good. Post 2 independently: "推荐牛肚汤！" and rates the pierogi "不踩雷但一般" (safe but unremarkable). A commenter confirms having gone and liked it. Four or five branches per Google. ~150 RMB per person for a very full meal, large portions (commenter, Jul 2026) — 小红书 [posts 2, 4]
- **Restauracja Polska Czerwony Wieprz** — Polish; 5/5. 酸菜炖肘子 (sauerkraut-stewed pork knuckle) very large; beef pierogi dense and full of meat; ribs at neighbouring tables also large; prices low, "性价比很高". Walls hung with Polish-communist-era themed propaganda art, interior styled to the 1940s–50s — 小红书 [post 5]
- **Restauracja Zapiecek** — pierogi chain; 5/5. Ordered strawberry-cheese, strawberry, raspberry, mushroom and beef pierogi; sweet pierogi eaten with sour cream; pork ribs tender and heavily seasoned, generous; potato pancakes served with berry jam + sour cream (author found that pairing odd and used ketchup) — 小红书 [post 5]
- **Bar Mleczny Lindleya 14** — milk bar; 5/5. Very good value, canteen-like but you order at the counter, self-serve bread included; bacon scrambled eggs and sausage both good; breaded pork cutlet with mashed potato noted as tempting — 小红书 [post 5]
- **PINI grill** — steak; 4/6; **their flaki is good**; located in the Mokotów shopping centre, walkable shopping after — 小红书 [post 3]
- **Paros** — Greek; 4/6; the author has eaten here for six or seven years; **has moved** from the street behind the Palace of Culture to opposite the main train station; seafood and mixed grill platter both good — 小红书 [post 3]
- **Polish restaurant, name in image only** — 4/6; sweet-leaning; must-order 蜜汁排骨 (honey-glazed ribs), pork knuckle a specialty — 小红书 [post 3] — *possibly Podwale, whose honey-glaze ribs are named twice in reddit.md; unconfirmed*
- **Kawalek** and **CUKIERNIA OLSZA** — named on a 华沙实用指南 card image alongside GOŚCINIEC; no detail in post text — 小红书 [搜索 grid, 攻略]
